Gods Arena

Walk up to a god and step on the glowing circle in front of them to take their powers. Draw your own battlefield on the magic paper in the middle of the hall, then beat 5 waves of evil gods, ending with Kronos.

Move: WASD or arrow keys · Attack: J or Space · God power: K · Attacks aim at the nearest enemy automatically.
